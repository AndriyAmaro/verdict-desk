# Rules v2 research: choose on train, judge on test

> **Two things are called v2.** This study's "v2" is a set of tuned parameters for the v1 arguments (threshold, stop, take-profit); it did not clear the bar and was not adopted. The `v2` rules that record the live dossiers (`verdict_desk/agents_v2.py`, with RSI, MACD and Bollinger arguments) are a different rule set, measured in [`V2_DIAGNOSIS.md`](V2_DIAGNOSIS.md).

Generated by `python -m verdict_desk research BTCUSDT ETHUSDT SOLUSDT --out research/v2_train_test.json` on 2026-10-17. Data: 4,000 hourly candles per symbol from the public Binance spot mirror, hashed in the JSON so anyone can reproduce the series. Every number is a test on past data, not live performance.

## Protocol (fixed before looking at results)

1. Cut each series in time: first 60% train (2,400 candles), last 40% test (1,600).
2. Evaluate a fixed grid of 36 judge settings on train only (entry threshold 1.5, 2.0, 2.5, 3.0; stop 1.0, 1.5, 2.0 ATR; take-profit 2.0, 3.0, 4.0 ATR), fees 0.05% per side, at least 20 trades.
3. Pick the best train result by net profit, freeze it, and run it on test next to the live rules (v1) on the same segment.
4. v2 goes live only if it beats v1 **and** is net positive out of sample on most symbols.

Unit tests prove the choice depends on train data only (replacing the test segment with a different market does not change the chosen setting) and that v1's parameters are unchanged.

## Result

| Symbol | v1 on test (net, profit factor) | v2 chosen on train | v2 on test (net, profit factor) | Buy and hold on test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BTCUSDT | -9.54%, 0.83 | threshold 2.0, stop 2.0, take 2.0 (0 of 36 settings positive on train) | -2.05%, 0.95 | +27.97% |
| ETHUSDT | -9.77%, 0.83 | threshold 2.5, stop 1.0, take 4.0 (4 of 36 positive) | -8.80%, 0.88 | +29.80% |
| SOLUSDT | +3.53%, 1.07 | threshold 2.5, stop 1.5, take 4.0 (17 of 36 positive) | +13.08%, 1.27 | +39.42% |

**Verdict: v2 beat v1 out of sample on 3 of 3 symbols but was net positive on only 1 of 3, and none beat buy and hold. It does not clear the bar, so v1 stays the live rule set.**

## How to read it

- The honest headline is the process, not the return. The rules are a transparent baseline for an audit-grade trail; they are not a trading edge.
- Wider stops and higher thresholds lose less in this sample. That is a hypothesis for the next round, not a finding: one split, three symbols, 36 settings tried, a test window in which every asset rose 28% to 39%.
- SOL's +13.08% is the best single number and also the easiest to over-read: with 36 candidates, one good-looking result is expected by chance.
- BTC had no profitable setting on train at all, so its "chosen" setting is the least bad, not a good one.
- Funding and news do not exist in the historical series, so only price and volume rules were exercised.
